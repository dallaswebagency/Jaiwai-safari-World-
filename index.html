About Jawai

Where leopards roam free

Jawai sits in the Pali district of Rajasthan, between Udaipur and Jodhpur, around the vast Jawai Bandh dam. Granite hills rise out of open scrubland and leopards live among them, beside the Rabari herders who have shared this land for generations.

Jawai Safari World is run by owner Nitesh Lohor. There are no fenced gates here. You ride in an open 4x4 jeep with a local guide who knows every rock, cave and ridge.

Good to know

Common questions

Where is Jawai located?

Jawai sits in the Pali district of Rajasthan, right in the middle of Udaipur and Jodhpur. It wraps around the huge Jawai Bandh dam, and the closest main town nearby is Sumerpur.

How do I reach Jawai?

By train, the nearest major station is Jawai Bandh (JWB), just a few kilometres away, with Falna (FA) also nearby. By air, Udaipur Airport (approx. 150 km) and Jodhpur Airport (approx. 165 km) are the closest. By road, Jawai is well connected by state and national highways, roughly 2 to 3 hours by taxi from Udaipur or Jodhpur.

What makes Jawai unique?

Jawai is world-renowned for the peaceful coexistence of wild leopards and the local Rabari pastoralist community. Unlike wildlife sanctuaries or national parks, there are no boundary fences, safari gates or government forest permit systems. Leopards roam freely across the granite hills, often close to human settlements without conflict.

Is a leopard sighting in Jawai guaranteed?

Wildlife sightings can never be 100% guaranteed, but Jawai has one of the highest leopard densities and sighting success rates in India.

What other wildlife can I see in Jawai?

Besides leopards, you can spot mugger crocodiles near the reservoir, striped hyenas, sloth bears, jungle cats, blue bulls (nilgai) and over 200 species of migratory and resident birds, including flamingos in winter.
